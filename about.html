About

Hello! My name is Elvin. I am an economics and mathematics graduate from Penn State. I created this website to cover specific mechanism design topics that I learned throughout my undergrad. I was heavily inspired by EconGraphs by Chris Makler and Probability Distribution Explorer by Justin Bois.

I am not a programmer, and I am even less a web designer/developer. I relied heavily on the use of AI coding agents like Codex and Claude Code to assist with the implementation of graphs, visuals, and the site in general. However, all the text was authored by me, and I designed the creative details of all the interactive features and graphs.

I am also not a mechanism design expert. I did my best to consult relevant papers and take notes from Auction Theory by Vijay Krishna and An Introduction to Mechanism Design by Börgers et al. to write the text for each page. The mechanism design literature is somewhat old, and the proofs and concepts in earlier fundamental papers, such as by Myerson, have been refined and modernized throughout the years. I did my best to synthesize earlier results with the modern language with the help of the aforementioned textbooks.

I would like the information on each page to be accessible to the most naive reader possible, but I must draw the line somewhere. I also wanted to make each page standalone, so that it could be read without drawing the reader into a web of prerequisite lessons, and short. This, unfortunately, means I had to draw the line quite high. The pages would be best understood by someone with experience in economics, game theory, probability theory, and undergraduate calculus/real analysis. However, even with little experience in these topics, I hope each page gives you the general intuition of the topic it covers.

If you have any suggestions or ideas for new pages, please do not hesitate to email me at elvinjiang2004@gmail.com.
