About

Hello! My name is Elvin. I am an economics and mathematics graduate from Penn State. I created this website to cover specific mechanism design topics that I studied throughout my undergrad, and ones that I am currently studying. I was heavily inspired by EconGraphs by Chris Makler and Probability Distribution Explorer by Justin Bois.

I am not a programmer, web developer, etc. I relied on the use of AI coding agents like Codex and Claude Code for implementation of graphs, visuals, and the site in general. However, all the text was authored by me, and I designed the creative details of all the interactive features and graphs.

If you have any suggestions or ideas for new pages, email me at elvinjiang2004@gmail.com.
