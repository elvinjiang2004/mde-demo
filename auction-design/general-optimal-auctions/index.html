General Optimal Auctions

This is the second page of a two-page overview of Myerson (1981)'s "Optimal Auction Design." This page will use results discussed in Regular Optimal Auctions, so it is recommended to read that page first.

In the single-item auction environment, Myerson (1981) gives the following result:

Theorem (): In general, a BIC, IR, revenue-maximizing mechanism is a second-price auction in ironed virtual values \(\bar{\phi}_i\) with reserve values \(r_i=\inf\{t\in T_i\mid\bar{\phi}_i(t)\geq 0\}\), \(i=1,\ldots,n\).

For each buyer \(i\) and fixed reports \(v_{-i}\), denote his "second price" as

\[ x_i(v_{-i})=\inf\left\{t\in T_i\ \middle| \begin{array}{l} \bar{\phi}_i(t)\geq 0,\\ \bar{\phi}_i(t)>\bar{\phi}_j(v_j)\quad\text{for every }j\lt i,\\ \bar{\phi}_i(t)\geq\bar{\phi}_j(v_j)\quad\text{for every }j\gt i \end{array} \right\}. \]

Denote the set of potential winners as

\[ W(v)=\left\{i\in\{1,\ldots,n\}\ \middle| \begin{array}{l} \bar{\phi}_i(v_i)\geq 0,\\ \bar{\phi}_i(v_i)=\max\{\bar{\phi}_j(v_j)\mid j\in\{1,\ldots,n\}\} \end{array} \right\}. \]

Then, equivalently, \((q^*,p^*)\) is an optimal mechanism where for \(i=1,\ldots,n\),

\[ q_i^*(v)=\begin{cases} 1 & \text{if }W(v)\neq\varnothing\text{ and }i=\min W(v),\\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}, \qquad p_i^*(v)=\begin{cases} x_i(v_{-i}) & \text{if }q_i^*(v)=1,\\ 0 & \text{if }q_i^*(v)=0 \end{cases}. \]
