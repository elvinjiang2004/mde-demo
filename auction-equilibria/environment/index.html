Environment

One object is to be sold. There are \(n\geq 2\) risk-neutral buyers. Buyers have independent private values (IPV) \(V_i\sim F_i\), \(i=1,\ldots,n\). We assume that each \(F_i\) has a continuous, strictly positive density \(f_i\) on its support \([\underline{v}_i,\overline{v}_i]\).

In any auction game, each buyer \(i\) privately observes his realized value \(v_i\), and then all buyers simultaneously submit their bids \(x_i\). How the winner and the price paid by each buyer are determined depends on the auction format. Ties are broken uniformly.
