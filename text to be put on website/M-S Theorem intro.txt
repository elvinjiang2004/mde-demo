A seller has an object that she may sell to a buyer. The seller has value C~F_s(c) and the buyer has value V~F_b(v). We assume F_s has continuous, strictly positive density f_s on its support [c_,cbar], and, likewise, the buyer has continuous, strictly positive density f_b on its support [v_,vbar]. We also assume the intersection of the supports is also an interval, that is, it is not empty and not a single point. Each of the buyer and seller observe his and her value v,c but not the other's. We want to find a <em>direct</em> bargaining mechanism $(q(v,c), p(v,c))$ in this environment that satisfies
1. (Bayesian) Incentive Compatibility (IC): Given the seller reports her true value, it is optimal for the buyer to do the same, and vice versa. 
2. Individual Rationality (IR): Both the buyer and seller have a weakly positive expected payoff after observing their values.
3. (Weak, ex-ante) Budget Balance (BB): The trade does not need to be subsidized by an outside party in expectation.
4. (Ex-post) Pareto Efficiency (PE): The mechanism maximizes welfare at every realized value profile. In other words, the person who values the object most always ends up with it.
In the interactive demo, try to design a direct bargaining mechanism that satisfies these four properties, or choose from a list of preset mechanisms. Here, V,C~i.i.d.Uniform[0,1]. Note the demo forces a discontinuous trade probability function, which may cause some small errors in the reported diagnostic values.
